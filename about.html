About UVIS Group

Engineering built around evidence, responsibility and real-world conditions.

UVIS Group is a Burundi-based engineering company providing technical services in geotechnical engineering, engineering design and experimental concrete testing.

Our purpose

We help project teams make technically sound decisions before uncertainty becomes cost.

Construction quality depends on what happens below the surface, inside the material and behind the calculations. Our role is to generate reliable information, interpret it correctly and turn it into practical engineering guidance.

We work with contractors, architects, government agencies, donors and project owners who require professional technical support, documented results and recommendations they can act on.

Our principles

What guides our work

01

Accuracy
